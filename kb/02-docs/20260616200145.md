## Context

Mutations in the porcelain apply immediately and irreversibly. Agents have no way to confirm what a `new`, `set`, or `link` would produce before committing it, leading to exploratory probing and post-hoc corrections.

## Decision

Any planned mutation — or a batch of mutations — can be **previewed** with a `--dry-run` flag. The preview produces the **same review-summary artifact** the mutation would generate on apply, without writing anything to the store.

Concretely:
- `ldoc new --dry-run ...` emits a review-summary-shaped output showing the doc that *would* be created (rendered fields, resolved edges).
- `ldoc set --dry-run ...` emits what the doc *would* look like after the update.
- `ldoc link --dry-run ...` emits the edge(s) that *would* be added.
- A batch of operations passed together preview as a single review summary covering all planned additions and revisions.

The preview format reuses the review-summary shape (`## Additions`, `## Revisions`, `## Minor Alterations`) rather than inventing a separate diff view. This means reviewers — human or agent — can read the same format whether reviewing a past changeset or previewing a future one.

## Consequences

- Agents can verify intent before committing: "does this `ldoc new` produce what I expect?" is answerable without writing.
- Ties the porcelain's preview affordance directly to the review-summary format — one format to learn, not two.
- Reduces post-hoc `ldoc set` corrections and the validation noise they generate.
- The dry-run output is NOT written to the review ledger; it is ephemeral output only.
