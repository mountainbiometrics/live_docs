## Constraint

A heading is a signpost whose only assertion is the grouping of its children. The signpost role stays structural. A doc has it by having children, whatever its type.

Docs that synthesize an overview, list entry points, recombine a subtree, rank a listing, or walk cascade keep keying off descendants. They do not key off the heading type.

## Why

[[20261007193650]] Any type can head its children. That is the hierarchy the store already keeps. The heading type names only the parent with no claim of its own, which is the standard this compromise sets.
