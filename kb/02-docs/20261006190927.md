## Goal

A reader, usually an agent, can tell from the line a doc gets in any listing or search result whether the doc is something to take into consideration: whether the person asked for it or only let it happen, whether it is a rule or a guideline, and whether it is current or deprecated.

## Why

Listings give a doc's title, its id, maybe its type, and maybe its summary, so an agent has to fetch the entire doc and make sense of it just to learn whether it matters to the task. A line reading "Decision #30" looks important, enforceable, and true; "Deprecated: Decision #30" does not, and neither does "Incidental: Decision #30".
