## Context

When a cascade check runs after a doc change, the check must produce a verdict for each dependent. The vocabulary and logging behavior of those verdicts need to be defined.

## Decision

Per dependent, cascade emits one of four verdicts: `inconsequential` | `cascade` | `incompatible` | `context-request`. Every verdict is recorded regardless of type. Propagation stops at `inconsequential`; `context-request` is used when the judge cannot confidently determine impact.

## Consequences

`inconsequential` as the explicit norm (rather than "no verdict") prevents silent false-negatives. `context-request` guards against the worst failure mode: a judge that quietly marks things inconsequential when it lacks the context to know (P2). Recording all verdicts creates an audit trail that gardening passes can mine to detect patterns (P6 — the system maintains itself). `incompatible` triggers immediate human attention; `cascade` propagates to dependents of dependents.
