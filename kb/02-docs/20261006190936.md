## Requirement

Listings and search results surface the most intentional docs first: the claims the person asked for or chose come ahead of claims that were only let happen. Material that exists only for reference's sake stays out of the way ([[20260717210152]]).

## Why

This is the ordering half of showing each doc's standing ([[20261006190927]]): a reader meets the docs that most need to be taken into consideration before the rest.
