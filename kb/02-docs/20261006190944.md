## Decision

A doc carrying `realization` ([[20261006190943]]) may also carry:

- `realization_refs` — a list of anchors into the implementation: file paths, symbols, URLs.
- `realization_verified` — the date or commit at which someone last checked the realization against those anchors.

Anchors are pointers, not edges: they point out of the store and cascade never walks them.

## Why

Implementation details belong in docs as pointers when people want them ([[20261006190934]]). Anchors are the pointers an agent follows to check whether a claim is built, and the verified stamp says how recent that check is.
