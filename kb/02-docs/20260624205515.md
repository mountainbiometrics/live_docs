## Decision

`connectionsHtml` renders below the doc body on every focused doc (and on composed member sections):

- **Outgoing →** — `belongs_to`, `requires`, `relates`, `provenance`, `superseded_by`, and `members (belongs_to →)` (except on signposts, where members render separately).
- **← Incoming** — reverse groups: required by, related from, cited by, supersedes. `belongs_to`-incoming is omitted here when it duplicates the member list.

Each entry is a resolved `linkHtml` with tooltip and `edgeBadge`. Empty sides show "none".
