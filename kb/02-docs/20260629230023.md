The two reverse hard edges — reverse belongs_to (children) and reverse requires (required_by) — must be named and described separately. They were previously conflated under a single 'dependents' label, but their semantics are fundamentally different: children are structural members of a parent (hierarchy), while required_by docs are logical dependents (the web of influence). This plan establishes the canonical vocabulary for each direction and documents where each term is appropriate.
