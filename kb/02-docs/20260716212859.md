## Context

An earlier design added a `keywords` frontmatter field — a flat, optional, non-inherited, ungoverned list of search synonyms, meant as a findability axis distinct from the governed `domain` facet ([[20260623233935]]). In practice that distinction never earned its keep: the field was rarely populated, never meaningfully governed despite garden-refine nominally curating it, and any real synonym need overlapped what `domain` or plain body prose already covered. It was a second axis answering a question `domain` + full-text search already answered well enough.

## Decision

Doc findability rests on:

- **`domain`** — the governed, filterable business-grouping facet ([[20260615203839]])
- **full-text search** — `ldoc find` matches title, label, and body

The former `keywords` field is retired — a pure subtraction, not a rename into another axis. That overturns [[20260623233935]].

A wishlist item for a store-hosted Integrated Lexicon (canonical terms, aliases, definitions) is a separate, unbuilt exploration of deliberate vocabulary. This retirement neither adopts nor blocks that idea; the lexicon is not a renamed stand-in for the retired field.

## Consequences

- Faceted filtering uses `domain` and `scope`; term search uses title, label, and body.
- garden-refine curates surface quality: titles, summaries, body shape, and schema normalization.
