## Statement

A document has **high cohesion** when everything in it relates to one concept. Parts that do not serve that concept do not belong in the doc.

## Why

High cohesion is a coding principle applied to documentation ([[20260625222626]]). Inside a well-factored module, the pieces stick together around one idea; the same test applies to a doc.

It **supports** single responsibility ([[20260615100003]]) — when content coheres around one concept, the doc is far more likely to have one reason to change — but the claims are distinct. Cohesion asks whether the *parts belong together*. Single responsibility asks whether the doc has *one reason to change*. A doc can fail either test independently: tangential asides break cohesion without adding a second change-reason; a tightly on-topic doc can still encode two responsibilities.

The repair for low cohesion is the same discipline as for overloaded scope: split or cut until every remaining part relates to the one concept.
