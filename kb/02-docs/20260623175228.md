## Component

**shard-clipping** is a pipeline stage that sits **between promote (gate 1) and ingest-reference (gate 2)** — hence "gate 1.5." It is **invoked automatically by ingest-reference** as that skill's first sub-step (and can also be run standalone). Its single job is to decide whether a promoted raw clipping is **one** gate-2 unit or **many**.

## Behavior

- For a **small / atomic-enough** clipping, shard-clipping is a **pass-through**: the raw flows unchanged into normal gate-2 ingest.
- For an **oversized, multi-concept** clipping (a Constitution, a long memo, a sprawling RFC), it gathers the verbatim passages for each over-arching concept cluster into **child raw clippings left pending**, and the ingest episode ends. The children are ingested later, each as its own gate-2 episode by the backlog loop.

This division embodies `[[20260623175215]]` (Divide the unit of work, not the phases): shard-clipping shrinks the *unit* — it never splits ingest into a plan-phase and an apply-phase. Comprehension and synthesis stay fused per shard.

## Where it lives

It is a stage of the inbox/ingest pipeline (`[[20260618230224]]`), refining the two-gate model of `[[20260616181832]]` (Inbox Pipeline) with an intermediate decision point. The mechanics of how children carry provenance and raw-tier pointers, how the recursion terminates, and how cross-shard duplicates are reconciled are decided in the docs that require this component.
