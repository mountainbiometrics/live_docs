## Requirement

A claim can record whether it was chosen or given: chosen internally as a self-assigned rule, or given — forced on the system by something external, or following from technology accepted elsewhere as part of a trade-off. The distinction applies to many kinds of claims, not only constraints.

## Why

Knowing that something is a technical constraint, bound by technology that was accepted elsewhere as part of a trade-off, is different from knowing it is an organizational constraint, bound by a standard or principle that was set by choice. The first moves when the trade-off is revisited; the second moves when the standard does ([[20260707170659]]).
