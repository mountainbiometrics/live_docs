## Requirement

Every normative doc, a claim about how things should be, says whether it binds as a rule or as a guideline. Deviating from a rule is a conflict; deviating from a guideline is allowed with a stated reason.

## Why

The line between "required" and "recommended" was being lost. There is an important difference between a rule and a guideline, even when the difference is subtle, and a reader deciding whether a doc constrains its work needs to see which one it is ([[20261006190927]]).
