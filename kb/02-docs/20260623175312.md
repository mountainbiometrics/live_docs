## Constraint

Because shard-clipping can be re-invoked on a child raw (a child may itself still be oversized), the recursion must be **guaranteed to terminate**. Three independent floors bound it; any one is sufficient to stop a given branch:

1. **Volume base case.** When a clipping's estimated concept count is **under the ~50-atomic-doc floor**, it fits one gate-2 unit; shard-clipping returns a pass-through verdict and the branch halts. This is the normal terminating case — and, because volume is the sole sharding trigger (`[[20260623184844]]`), it is also why most clippings (including dense multi-subject notes) never shard at all.
2. **No-progress guard.** A clipping may still be over the volume floor yet resist a clean split: if it **cannot be cut into at least two smaller shards** — each strictly smaller and still wholly covering its passages — shard-clipping passes it through unchanged rather than tearing a single concept across shards or producing a trivial child (which would recurse forever on the same content). No split that fails to make the children smaller is allowed.
3. **Hard depth cap of 2 levels.** Sharding may descend at most **2 levels**, tracked by the `shard_depth` field on each raw clipping. At the cap, a clipping is ingested as-is even if still over the floor — the cap is an absolute backstop against pathological recursion.

The `shard_depth` field is the explicit counter that makes floor 3 enforceable and auditable. This constraint guards the shard-clipping stage (`[[20260623175228]]`); its floors are expressed in the same volume terms as the sharding trigger (`[[20260623184844]]`), not in topical-disjointness terms.
