## Constraint

Because shard-clipping can be re-invoked on a child raw (a child may itself still be oversized), the recursion must be **guaranteed to terminate**. Three independent floors bound it; any one is sufficient to stop a given branch:

1. **Atomic-enough base case.** When a clipping is judged small / single-concept enough for one gate-2 unit, shard-clipping returns a pass-through verdict and the branch halts. This is the normal terminating case.
2. **No-progress guard.** If the material **will not separate into at least two concept clusters**, shard-clipping passes it through unchanged rather than producing a single trivial child (which would recurse forever on the same content). No split that fails to make progress is allowed.
3. **Hard depth cap of 2 levels.** Sharding may descend at most **2 levels**, tracked by the `shard_depth` field on each raw clipping. At the cap, a clipping is ingested as-is even if still large — the cap is an absolute backstop against pathological recursion.

The `shard_depth` field is the explicit counter that makes floor 3 enforceable and auditable. This constraint guards the shard-clipping stage (`[[20260623175228]]`).
