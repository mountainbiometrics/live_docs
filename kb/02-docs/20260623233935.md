## Context

`ldoc find` matches a doc by its title, label, and body, and filters by `scope`/`domain`. A searcher who reaches for a synonym or an alias the author did not happen to use in those fields cannot surface the doc. `domain` does not fill this gap: it is a governed grouping facet, not a bag of search terms.

## Decision

`keywords` is a distinct frontmatter field: a **flat list of search synonyms and aliases** that exists purely for *findability*. Like `domain`, it is flat — **not inherited** through the hierarchy and **not a cascade edge**; it is optional, and an omitted field means the empty list. Unlike `domain`, it carries **no governance** — keywords are free-text terms a searcher might plausibly type, added freely with no vocabulary discipline and no justify-its-existence test.

`keywords` and `domain` are **separate axes that must not overlap**. `domain` is the **governed, filterable grouping facet** — a curated business/problem concern that must justify its existence (see [[20260615203839]], [[20260624185845]]). `keywords` makes a doc *findable* under terms a reader would search for. The partition is clean: **a term that is, or matches, an existing domain belongs as a `domain` tag — not a keyword.** Keywords are for the search aliases that are *not* domains. A doc can carry domains, keywords, or neither, but the same term should not be duplicated across both.

## Consequences

- `ldoc new` and `ldoc set` accept `--keywords` (comma-separated, replace semantics — the same shape as `--domain`).
- `ldoc find` includes `keywords` in its **term match** alongside title/label/body — keywords are an extra surface a search term *might* match. They are **not a filter facet**: there is no `--keyword` filter, because anything worth filtering by belongs in `domain`. `domain` (and `scope`) are the only faceted-filter axes.
- `validate` treats keywords as free-text: trimmed and de-duped per doc, with no reference resolution — there is nothing for a keyword to point at.
- Because there is no governance bar, curating keywords is surface-quality work, not a structural decision; routing a keyword that is really a domain to the `domain` field is part of that curation.
