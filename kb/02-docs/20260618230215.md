How docs connect: the typed-edge vocabulary, what each edge means behaviorally, and the graph rules.

The entry point is `[[20260617144634]]` (Edge Type Vocabulary), which names the four edge types — belongs_to, requires, relates, provenance — and what each is for. `[[20260617144542]]` (Name Edge Types After Behavior) gives the rationale behind that vocabulary: edges are named for their behavioral semantics, not their conceptual category. `[[20260617144637]]` (Rename References to Provenance) settles that provenance and relates are distinct and not collapsible, recording the field rename that fixed it. `[[20260617144556]]` (Graph Cycles Are Legal) governs the graph rules: acyclicity is a per-edge-type constraint, not a graph-level rule.
