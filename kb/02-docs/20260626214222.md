Captures the abstract design whys surfaced while refining the skills after the dogfood post-mortem, separated from the operational mechanics (verdict vocabularies, new garden phases) that live in skill prose. The load-bearing new whys: supersession detection must reason over the topological cluster rather than rely on lexical search (and a system-changing initiative that supersedes nothing is a red flag); and agent-facing skills must be self-contained — never referencing the internal KB — because they travel to repos that lack it. Provenance-based grouping masquerading as structure is called out as a corollary of the hierarchy being the system's mental model.
