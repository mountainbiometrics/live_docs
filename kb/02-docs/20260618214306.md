## Decision

`domain` and `scope` are **flat, top-level** frontmatter lists, **omitted when empty**, rather than nested under a `tags` mapping.

## Rationale

Nesting them under `tags` gave **no benefit** and rendered badly in Obsidian. Flattening makes both fields directly visible and queryable at the top level; **omitting the empty case** offsets the verbosity cost so unclassified docs stay clean. This composes with the omit-empty-lists convention and the domain-tag grouping decision.
