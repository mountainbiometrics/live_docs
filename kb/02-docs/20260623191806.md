## Decision

On successful exit from any ldoc command that mutates a **viewer-exported** store tier, the porcelain **automatically rebuilds** `build/viewer.html`. Covered mutators include doc commands (`new`, `set`, `edit`, `link`, `unlink`, `history`, `rm`), review commands (`review new`, `review sign`), and `session close` (which mints a review). Additional mutators that change exported tiers follow the same rule when those tiers are added — ownership of *what* is exported stays with the export/surfaces, not this decision.

## Scope and exclusions

Read-only commands, `validate`, `reindex`, inbox/raw/promote, and `--dry-run` previews do **not** trigger a rebuild. `ldoc viewer` does not double-build. Mutations confined to tiers that are **not** in the viewer export do not trigger rebuild.

## Opt-out and observability

Auto-rebuild is **on by default** and **silent by default** (skill episodes invoke porcelain many times per episode). Per invocation: `ldoc --no-viewer …`. Globally: `LIVEDOCS_AUTO_VIEWER=0`. Verbose notice: `LIVEDOCS_AUTO_VIEWER_VERBOSE=1`.

## Non-gating failure mode

Rebuild failures are **swallowed** — a stale viewer must never cause a porcelain mutation to fail. Rebuild logic lives in the shared scripts layer; the HTML artifact stays a dumb read-only projection.

## Rationale

At typical store sizes rebuild is sub-second, so per-command regeneration keeps the reader fresh without a manual `ldoc viewer` step. Batch workflows can opt out and rebuild once at the end when that is cheaper.
