## Decision

On successful exit from any ldoc command that mutates **docs/** or **reviews/** (the tiers exported into viewer.html), the porcelain **automatically rebuilds** `build/viewer.html`. Covered mutators: doc commands (`new`, `set`, `edit`, `link`, `unlink`, `history`) and review commands (`review new`, `review sign`).

## Scope and exclusions

Read-only commands, `validate`, `reindex`, inbox/raw/promote, and `--dry-run` previews do **not** trigger a rebuild. `ldoc viewer` does not double-build. Mutations confined to raw/, inbox/, or `.index/` alone do not trigger rebuild — those tiers are not in the viewer export.

## Opt-out and observability

Auto-rebuild is **on by default** and **silent by default** (skill episodes invoke porcelain many times per episode). Per invocation: `ldoc --no-viewer …`. Globally: `LIVEDOCS_AUTO_VIEWER=0`. Verbose notice: `LIVEDOCS_AUTO_VIEWER_VERBOSE=1`.

## Non-gating failure mode

Rebuild failures are **swallowed** — a stale viewer must never cause a porcelain mutation to fail. Rebuild logic lives in the shared scripts layer; the HTML artifact stays a dumb read-only projection.

## Rationale

At typical store sizes rebuild is sub-second, so per-command regeneration keeps the reader fresh without a manual `ldoc viewer` step. Batch workflows can opt out and rebuild once at the end when that is cheaper.
