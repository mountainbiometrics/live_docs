## Decision

The type is named heading.

A heading carries intent. Force, realization, and imposed_by are forbidden. It expects no requires edge. Scope and domain stay optional: a heading may declare either, and most inherit.

Validate requires a heading to have at least one living child. It does not require the body to link those children. Membership stays on the children's belongs_to edges.

The name is heading because it is slightly unfamiliar. It is used when a doc is needed as a heading over its children.

## Why

[[20261007193651]] makes the claimless parent a type a checker can read. [[20261007193652]] keeps every other signpost behavior on descendants, so a decision or a requirement can still head its children. The facet row follows from what a heading asserts: who asked for the grouping, and nothing that would make the doc a norm, a buildable claim, or a why-chain node.
