## Decision

Every mutation **double-writes**: it appends a per-doc **history entry** (the doc's own trail) *and* a **WAL line** to the open session's record. The WAL is the **source of truth** for the session's review — the review is built from the WAL, not by scanning every doc's history.

## Why a WAL, not just history

A per-doc history entry lives on the doc. A **deletion** removes the doc, so it leaves no history — the change would otherwise be invisible. The WAL captures it: a deletion line records the dead doc's label/type and the edges that were stripped, so the change is auditable and, unlike current-state scanning, **replayable**.

## Entry shape

Each WAL line records the operation, the target ref, the **change_type(s)** it touched (a list — see the change-type taxonomy), and the note. The timestamp-window (`--since`) path is retained as a fallback for building a review without a session.
