## Context

Docs vary in authority. Some come from an explicit request or choice by the person; others emerged as agent articulations, organic notes, or unconfirmed suggestions. The store records where a claim came from and what the person did about it separately: the `provenance` edge records the source, and `intent` ([[20261006190938]]) records whether the person requested the claim, chose it, or let it happen.

## Decision

Every doc should record its source or derivation via a `provenance` edge (or a reference doc's `--source`). A doc with no recorded provenance is `intent: incidental`.

A provenance edge alone never raises a claim's intent:

- Archiving a session digest and pointing `provenance` at it does not make every claim extracted from it `requested` or `chosen`. Those rungs need an `intent_basis` citing the person's own words or choice for this claim ([[20261006190939]]).
- Silence is not ratification ([[20260723223658]]): an agent-proposed claim the person did not explicitly confirm stays `incidental` even when a provenance edge exists.

## Consequences

Gardening can surface incidental docs as candidates for the person to ratify or delete (`ldoc ls --intent incidental`). Agent-facing skills must operationalize this rule; KB recording alone does not reach external agents ([[20260624225658]]).
