## What this is

The **Integrated Lexicon** is a store-hosted **sibling system**: its own box of human-ratified vocabulary records (canonical term, definition, optional context, aliases, similar terms). It is **not** a typed-doc subsystem and **not** a cascade/contract layer. Terms are outside the knowledge graph for cascade and validate-as-docs purposes.

Humans are the primary audience for lexical entries; LLMs remain primary consumers of docs. Soft sync runs both ways — the KB surfaces candidate language from real use; the lexicon guides wording without mandating rewrites. Terms are defined from language already accruing in the store, not from a hollow seed list.

The lexicon is a deliberate curated vocabulary sibling. It is **not** a like-for-like replacement for the removed `keywords` frontmatter field ([[20260716212859]]).
