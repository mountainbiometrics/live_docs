## Statement

The `ldoc` porcelain — and the scripts behind it — is the **sole authority** for the store's schema and the **sole mutation path**. External integrations (notably Obsidian plugins) must be treated as **read-only** display and navigation layers.

## Constraint

Any external feature that **writes back** to frontmatter — alias writers, implied-edge writers, property editors — must stay **off**. Such writers churn frontmatter and history and fight the tooling's schema authority, corrupting the invariants `ldoc` enforces and polluting the change-trail.

## Rationale

A single mutation path is what makes the store's invariants enforceable and its history honest: every change passes through one validated gate. This generalizes tool-agnosticism — tool-agnostic means no tool is *required*, and equally that no external tool may silently *own* the data. Read-only consumption (graph view, navigation, display plugins) is encouraged; write-back is not.
