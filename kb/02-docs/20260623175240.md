## Decision

When shard-clipping splits an oversized clipping, each **shard** is a **gathering of verbatim source passages** for **one over-arching concept cluster** — not a paraphrase, summary, or rewrite. The shards carve the source by *concept*, not by document position.

Properties of the gathering:

- **Verbatim** — passages are copied exactly from the source; never rephrased. Rephrasing is synthesis, and synthesis belongs to gate-2 ingest of the child, not to the split.
- **Non-contiguous** — a single concept's passages may be scattered across the source; a shard pulls them together regardless of position.
- **May overlap** — the same passage may appear in more than one shard when it bears on more than one concept cluster. Overlap is permitted; it is not an error.

## The one hard rule: full coverage

Every passage in the source must land in **at least one** shard. Full coverage is the single invariant the split must satisfy — nothing in the original may be dropped. Overlap (a passage in ≥2 shards) is tolerated; a gap (a passage in 0 shards) is not.

Tolerating overlap while forbidding gaps is deliberate: duplicate material across shards is later reconciled by concept-matching and gardening (`[[20260623175228]]` requires this), whereas lost material is unrecoverable.
