## Statement

**Silence is not ratification.** When an agent proposes a claim and the person does not contradict it, that is not confirmation. The claim stays `intent: incidental` ([[20261006190938]]) unless the person explicitly requests or chooses it, and the evidence for that goes in the doc's `intent_basis` ([[20261006190939]]).

## Why

Agents fail at tracking what came directly from the person. The observed failure mode: an agent articulates a suggestion, the person stays silent or merely continues, and a later agent treats the store as proof that "we have to do it this way because you said so." That fabricates authority.

`intent` records what the person did, not how important a claim feels, and a provenance edge does not raise it ([[20260615100014]]). A working-session digest as provenance does not elevate every agent articulation in that session. Skills must operationalize this rule; KB recording alone does not reach external agents ([[20260624225658]]).
