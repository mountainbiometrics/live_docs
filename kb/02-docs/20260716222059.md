## Intent

Introduce a store-hosted Integrated Lexicon as a sibling system — not a typed-doc subsystem and not a cascade/contract layer. It is its own box of human-ratified term records. The KB suggests candidates; the lexicon guides wording without mandating rewrites (soft sync both ways). Humans are the primary audience for lexical entries; LLMs remain primary for docs.

## Settled design

1. **Unnumbered sibling box** `lexicon` (default `kb/lexicon/` under `base`), same class as `reviews/`/`sessions/` — outside the knowledge graph for cascade/validate-as-docs.
2. **Identity = term slug** (plus optional `context` for homonyms). No opaque timestamp IDs. Rename = delete/recreate; references are not backfilled.
3. **Paths**: ubiquitous `lexicon/<term>.md`; contextual `lexicon/<context>/<term>.md`; multi-word terms use kebab-case lowercase.
4. **Term record fields** (omit empty): term, definition, context (optional), allowed_aliases, restricted_aliases, similar_terms, created. No status, cascade, or history in v1.
5. **Soft wiki-style term links** — broken links allowed and useful; renames do not rewrite old references.
6. **Display**: Title Case; with context `(Context) Term`; machine form `context/term`.
7. **Viewer (target)**: resolve/auto-link terms, hover definitions, own Lexicon view (not Catalog tab), cheap usage stats via backlinks/search.
8. **Porcelain (target)**: read/write helpers for lexicon term CRUD/list/show.
9. Lexicon is **not** a like-for-like replacement for the removed `keywords` field.
10. Skills for identify/add/align terms, formatter auto-link, suggest-on-edit remain wishlist.
