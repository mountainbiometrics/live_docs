## Context

Docs need metadata expressing how settled or authoritative they are. A single "adoption" field was considered, but it conflates two orthogonal dimensions.

## Decision

Adoption is split into two fields: `level` (provenance/settledness: incidental → trial → preference → requirement) and `state` (temporal target: actual | target). These are independent axes and must remain separate fields.

## Consequences

A doc can be `level: requirement, state: target` (this is what we want but haven't achieved yet) or `level: incidental, state: actual` (this is happening but wasn't a deliberate decision). Collapsing them into one field would make these combinations inexpressible or require awkward compound values. The separation enables precise drift detection: a `state: target` doc that hasn't become `actual` after some time is a trackable gap (P2, P3).

## Correction

The `state` field no longer exists. The schema migration (2026-06-17) absorbed `state: actual|target` into the `status` enum: `status: living` replaces `state: actual`, and `status: target` replaces `state: target`. That enum was later narrowed in turn ([[20260617212538]]): `level` was replaced by `intent` ([[20261006190938]]), and whether a claim is realized moved out of `status` into its own `realization` field ([[20261006190943]]), retiring `target`.
