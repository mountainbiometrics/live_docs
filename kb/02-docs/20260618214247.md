## Use-case

live_docs is developed by **documenting its own evolution with its own tools**. The spec and its first instance coexist in one repo, so design conflicts surface early — while the design is still cheap to change.

## Rationale

**Eating our own cooking** is the cheapest way to find where the design breaks: every rough edge is felt directly by the people building the system, not reported second-hand. New capabilities (the inbox pipeline, reconcile-changes) are **validated by immediately using them on this repo**, so a flaw shows up on the first real run rather than after release.
