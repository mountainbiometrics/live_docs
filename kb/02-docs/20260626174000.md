## What gardening is

Gardening is the **store maintenance pass** — the counterpart to ingest. Where ingest brings knowledge in and originates doc placements, gardening refines those placements and improves store quality with full-store context. It is **not** a mechanical formatter or a diff applier; it is an agentic judgment pass.

The governing decision ([[20260623233922]]) is that gardening is realized as a **thin dispatcher over a family of single-purpose phases** (decompose, collapse, hierarchy, domains, refine, integrity). The dispatcher owns the whole episode — one START, the routing decision, one cascade-check over the union of changes, one review — while each phase owns its job and reports only the ids it changed.

## Two governing principles

**Placement vs. navigability are independent axes** ([[20260624172702]]). Every doc in an overgrown branch can be correctly placed and the branch can still be a navigability failure. Placement correctness and tree navigability each require independent maintenance; fixing one does not automatically fix the other.

**What makes a good `belongs_to` placement** is defined once ([[20260623233906]]) and consumed verbatim by both ingest and gardening — so the two cannot drift. The authoritative placement policy: prefer the nearest coherent signpost, one primary parent, the right-sized branch, and defer rather than force a bad home. Gardening has the advantage of full-store context that ingest lacks.

## The evaluative dimension

Gardening's highest-value contribution is **judging what should stop existing** ([[20260625222723]]). Cataloguing what is present is the low-value part; deciding what is idiotic, redundant, or intention-lost is the high-value part. A gardening pass that only moves things is incomplete; it should also ask whether things should exist at all.
