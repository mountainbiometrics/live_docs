Records the design for shard-clipping, conceptually gate 1.5 of the inbox pipeline, sitting between promote (gate 1) and ingest-reference (gate 2). It is now implemented and out of draft.

## Problem
The gate-2 ingest agent holds one context across the whole decomposition of a raw clipping — it must comprehend the entire source AND synthesize every atomic doc. For a large, multi-concept clipping (a Constitution, a long memo, an RFC) one context cannot hold both jobs; the decomposition step degrades exactly where atomicity is produced.

## Core principle — divide the unit of work, not the phases
A plan-phase/apply-phase split is rejected as a false economy: either the plan must serialize everything (the writer becomes redundant) or the writer gets latitude and needs the comprehension nuance the plan dropped (re-comprehending from a lossy summary). Comprehension and synthesis are inseparable for one conceptual unit. Instead, shrink the thing gate 2 operates on so one context holds both.

## The shard decision
shard-clipping reasons about over-arching concept COUNT and structure — never word count. Atomic-enough clippings pass through unchanged (the common case, a near-no-op). Oversized/multi-concept clippings are sharded. Bias toward pass-through; sharding has real cost.

## Shards are verbatim passage-gatherings
A shard gathers the verbatim source passages for one over-arching concept cluster, pulled from anywhere in the document. Passages may be non-contiguous and may overlap between shards. Words are never rephrased — selection and grouping are the only freedom. The one hard rule is FULL COVERAGE: every passage lands in at least one shard.

## Children left pending; ingest ends
On a shard verdict, ingest-reference leaves the children as pending raw clippings and ends — it never enumerates or loops over them, so one context never re-accumulates the bulk sharding exists to shed. The backlog loop over 'ldoc raw list --pending' ingests them later, each as its own independent gate-2 episode.

## Cross-shard duplicates tolerated, not prevented
Order does not matter. Duplicate concepts across shards are reconciled by map-concepts-to-docs' concept-matching plus the gardening passes — the machinery that already exists for this. So no serialization is needed.

## Termination floor
Recursion terminates via three floors: atomic-enough verdict halts (base case), a no-progress guard (won't separate into >=2 clusters -> pass through), and a hard depth cap of 2 levels tracked by the shard_depth field.

## Raw-tier mechanics
Each child carries a parent_raw path pointer (a raw-tier pointer, NOT a graph edge — raw files are not graph nodes) and inherits the parent's provenance unchanged. The parent raw stays the immutable whole-archive. Ingest-state is tracked hybrid (an ingested_at flag cross-checked against graph references); a sharded parent derives as [sharded] once its children are ingested.
