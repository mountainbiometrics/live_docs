Seven abstract principles and constraints from the user's prompts that motivated the garden redesign decisions. These are the conceptual layer underneath the implementation decisions already ingested (20260623233922, 20260623233935, 20260623233906, 20260623233949): (1) single responsibility means singular ownership, not just topic isolation; (2) tree placement correctness and tree navigability (branch-level) are independent axes; (3) agentic pipelines produce sporadic inconsistency and sampling-based QA is the correct response; (4) natural-language dispatchers shield users from internal sub-skill names and sub-skills are never entry points; (5) merge (dedup) and fold (cruft absorption) are distinct collapse operations with distinct preconditions; (6) shared prose includes are for shared text while sub-skills are for shared procedure; (7) doc-emitting skills should originate a semi-adequate starting placement rather than deferring all placement to gardening.
