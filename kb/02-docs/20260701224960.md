## Component

An open session is a **record on disk**, in a configurable `sessions/` box (parallel to `reviews/`). Keyed by session id, the record holds the session's settable **agent-recap summary** and its **change log** (the write-ahead log, or WAL). Persisting the session — not merely its id — is what makes in-progress sessions listable, resumable, and mergeable. It is distinct from the session *id pointer*, which stays ambient in the environment and is never a shared file.

## Consumed into the review at close

`session close` rolls the record's WAL up into exactly **one** review and then **deletes the live record**. The raw WAL is preserved **losslessly** inside the review file, in a delimited block that `ldoc review` and the viewer do not render by default. Nothing is lost, but the durable artifact after close is the review, not the session record.
