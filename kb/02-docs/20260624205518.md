## Decision

The neighborhood graph is **always visible** in the Reader side panel (not a collapsible under-body widget). Expansion is **edge-aware**:

**1-hop** — focus plus direct neighbors across belongs_to / requires / relates / superseded_by (forward and reverse). Provenance is excluded. Docs with `type: reference` or `status: reference` are excluded (`[[20260717210152]]`).

**Optional 2-hop** — follows per-type rules rather than a uniform BFS:

- **belongs_to ancestors** — climb up to two generations (structural context)
- **belongs_to descendants** — descend one generation
- **requires / required_by** — hop once to the neighbor's immediate belongs_to parent (its scope)
- **relates** — no further hop
- **provenance** — not shown at all

**NODE_CAP 40** still applies; 1-hop neighbors are always kept. Nodes are styled by type icon and domain color (overridable via `[[20260710235233]]`). Clicking a node refocuses Reader.

## Why

A uniform BFS treats every edge as the same kind of "nearness." Edge types are not interchangeable — climbing the hierarchy, seeing a requirement's scope, and stopping at relates are different judgments. Edge-aware hops keep the graph informative instead of noisy; keeping it always on in a side panel makes exploration ambient without burying the body.
