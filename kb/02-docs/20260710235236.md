## Decision

Docs with **`type: reference`** or **`status: reference`** are treated as archived for navigation: they are omitted from the hierarchy tree, the neighborhood graph, and catalog-default listings. They remain in the export for link resolution and still appear in per-doc connection lists when something points at them.

## Why

Reference docs are frozen snapshots, not living claims. Letting them compete in the tree and graph dilutes the mental model the hierarchy is meant to show. Demotion keeps the navigational surfaces about what is current while preserving reachability when a living doc actually cites them.
