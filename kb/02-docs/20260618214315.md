## Decision

The distillation boxes live under a single **`kb/`** parent: `00-inbox/`, `01-raw/`, `02-docs/`. Boxes that are **not** a distillation stage sit alongside them **unnumbered** — for example process-state boxes such as `kb/reviews/` and `kb/sessions/`. Which sibling boxes exist is owned by the decisions that introduce them; this decision owns only the numbering rule.

## Rationale

Colocation under `kb/` **declutters the repo root**, and the numeric prefixes encode the **distillation gradient**: capture → accepted clipping → atomic doc. Process and sibling-system boxes carry no distillation stage, so they stay unnumbered rather than taking a prefix. The numbered-box shape is **borrowed from prior art** (a research-oriented knowledge base), chosen per the incidental-patterns principle — adopt the incidental convention while keeping our own schema and edge decisions intact.
