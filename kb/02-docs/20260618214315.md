## Decision

The input boxes live under a single **`kb/`** parent: `00-inbox/`, `01-raw/`, `02-docs/`. The reviews ledger is **`kb/reviews/`** — **unnumbered**, because it is *process state*, not a distillation stage.

## Rationale

Colocation under `kb/` **declutters the repo root**, and the numeric prefixes encode the **distillation gradient**: capture → accepted clipping → atomic doc. The numbered-box shape is **borrowed from prior art** (the schema-concept-learning-kb), chosen per the incidental-patterns principle — adopt the incidental convention while keeping our own schema and edge decisions intact.
