A heading is a doc type for a parent whose only assertion is the grouping of its children. The need is that the why-rule already excuses that kind of parent, and a checker can apply the excuse only when the parent is declared. Any type may still have children, and that structural role stays the signpost: overview, entry points, recombination, listings, and cascade keep following descendants. A heading carries intent, forbids force, realization, and imposed_by, expects no why-chain edge, and may set a scope or a domain. Validate requires at least one living child and does not require the body to link those children. The name is heading because it is slightly unfamiliar, so it is used when a doc is needed as a heading. A component, decision, or requirement that also makes a claim keeps its type and its why.
