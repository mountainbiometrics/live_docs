## Decision

`intent_basis` is a frontmatter string holding the evidence behind a doc's intent: the quote or citation (session, review, raw clipping, date) showing the person requested or chose the claim.

- It is required when `intent` is `requested` or `chosen`. The mutation that sets either value refuses to run without it, and validate reports an error for a doc that carries either value with no basis.
- It appears in `get` and `show`, and never in listings.

## Why

A mechanical check on the evidence for an intent above incidental keeps agents from raising a claim's intent on their own say-so ([[20260723223658]]). The check belongs in a frontmatter field with a clear name; a required `## Basis` section would put it in body text, which is the wrong place for it.
