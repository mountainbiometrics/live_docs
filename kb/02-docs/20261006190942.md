## Decision

Force ([[20261006190941]]) is named in the reason when a change touches a normative claim. It does not select the cascade verdict ([[20260615100012]]); the intent comparison does ([[20261006190940]]). A `must` the person never set is an incidental claim like any other.

## Why

A rule and a guideline differ ([[20261006190931]]), and the size of a departure is worth seeing in the report. It is not a mechanism for refusing the next thing the person said.
