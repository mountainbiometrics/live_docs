## Decision

When cascade-check finds a conflict with a normative claim, the claim's force ([[20261006190941]]) sets the verdict ([[20260615100012]]):

- a conflict with a `must` claim is `incompatible`;
- a conflict with a `should` claim is a `context-request`, asking for clarification.

The same split governs agents writing code: an agent may deviate from a `should` claim with a note giving its reason, and never deviates from a `must` claim without the person.

## Why

This is the difference between a rule and a guideline ([[20261006190931]]) applied where agents act on docs: deviating from a rule is a conflict, and deviating from a guideline is allowed with a reason.
