## Restatement

After failed reconcile/apply passes and reiterated apply-to-docs feedback, extraction and synthesis must prefer the nonrecoverable why-web — principles, constraints, requirements, and goals that justified choices — over piles of thin decision-restating docs. Decisions remain useful as modeling nodes that require those roots, but live_docs is a higher-resolution lens into why, not a replica of design/ADR docs. Implementation and even decisions are often recoverable; the shaping constraints and goals are not once the conversation ends. On authority: silence is not ratification — agent-suggested claims the user did not explicitly confirm stay `level: incidental`; `level` means claim settledness/authority, not "has a provenance edge." Durable labels prefer the user's vocabulary. These policies must live in skills (which travel) as well as the KB; skills are being refined in parallel to operationalize them.
