## The scenario

The people who need what a store holds are not all working from a checkout of it. A non-technical coworker wants to read what the design says. A new employee arrives. Someone is set up on a new workstation. Each of them needs an up-to-date copy of the store's knowledge, and getting one should not mean checking the store out and keeping that checkout current.

## What it asks of the system

A store runs on a shared host, and the people who need it hold its address. Reaching the store becomes a matter of being pointed at it rather than of holding a copy that can fall behind.
