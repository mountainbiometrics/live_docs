## Requirement

Each doc records, in its frontmatter, what the person did to bring its claim into the store: asked for it directly, chose it when presented with options, or let it happen when an agent proposed it. Tooling reads that record and shows it.

## Why

Agents in a consuming store started writing provenance notes into doc bodies so they could tell "this was asked for directly" from "an agent suggested this, and the user didn't refute it." A store may keep its own conventions and writing style, but this purpose belongs in the system itself: it is the whole point of a doc's level, and as body prose it is invisible to tooling and listings.
