## Decision

Lexicon term **`context`** answers: *which sense of this string?* It exists only for homonyms (e.g. `(Graph) Cascade` vs a ubiquitous Cascade). It is omitted when the term is ubiquitous.

Doc **`domain`** ([[20260615203839]]) answers a different question: *which business/problem concern is this doc about?* Domain is a governed, filterable facet on typed docs.

These axes must not be conflated. A term's `context` is not a domain tag; a doc's `domain` does not place or disambiguate lexicon entries.
