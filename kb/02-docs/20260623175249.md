## Decision

When shard-clipping returns a **shard verdict** (the clipping was split into children), ingest-reference **leaves the child raws pending and ends the episode**. It does **not** enumerate the children, and it does **not** loop over them to ingest them inline.

## Why ingest must end, not loop

The whole point of sharding is that one agent context could not hold both comprehension and synthesis of the oversized whole. If the same episode then looped over every child, that one context would **re-accumulate the bulk** it was trying to shed — defeating the split. Ending the episode guarantees each child gets a **fresh context**.

## Who ingests the children

The **backlog loop** over pending raws (`ldoc raw list --pending`) picks up each child later and ingests it as its **own gate-2 episode** — a normal, full ingest-reference run over that child's portion. Each child is comprehended and synthesized whole, in its own context, preserving the unit-of-work discipline (`[[20260623175228]]` requires the shard-clipping stage that produces these pending children).
