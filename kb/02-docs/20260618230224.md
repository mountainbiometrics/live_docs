How external material and session decisions enter the store: the async inbox, the batch ingest pass, the provenance metadata that travels with captured material, the gate-1.5 sharding step that keeps large clippings ingestable, and the docs-first/reconcile/apply workflows that materialize intent into atomic docs.

The framing principle is `[[20260615100016]]` (Ingest Gardening Core): ingest and gardening are foundational passes, not deferred features. The capture path runs through `[[20260616181805]]` (Async Inbox), which decouples capture from ingestion, and `[[20260616181832]]` (Inbox Pipeline), the drop-point plus batch ingest pass that drains it. Travelling along that path is provenance: `[[20260623162457]]` (Pipeline Provenance Metadata) records `origin`, `medium`, and `authored_at` at gate 0 and carries them losslessly to the raw clipping, and `[[20260623162513]]` (Three Distinct Timestamps) holds the invariant that `authored_at`, `captured`, and `imported` are three distinct timestamps the pipeline must preserve — the basis for staleness reasoning across heterogeneous sources. The intent-first workflows are anchored by `[[20260616181843]]` (Plan Changes Docs-First) — record intent in docs before building so the store leads reality — and realized by `[[20260618000147]]` (Apply-to-docs Skill), the skill that materializes a request or plan into the store, with `[[20260618000209]]` (Apply-to-docs Must Pause) requiring it to pause and warn when the blast radius is large. The governing rule across all of it is `[[20260618000251]]` (Understand Before Write): survey for compatible, conflicting, or superseded claims before any write. `[[20260618213505]]` (Reconcile Changes) handles the reverse direction — catching the store up to changes already made, recording them as living docs.

Between gate 1 (promote) and gate 2 (ingest) sits **shard-clipping (gate 1.5)**, the stage that keeps an oversized clipping ingestable. Its founding principle is `[[20260623175215]]` (Divide the Unit, Not the Phases): when one clipping overflows a single ingest context, shrink the unit of work and ingest each smaller unit whole — never split ingest into a plan-phase and an apply-phase, because comprehension and synthesis are inseparable. `[[20260623175228]]` (Shard-clipping Gate 1.5) is the stage itself: invoked automatically by ingest-reference, it decides whether a promoted raw is one gate-2 unit or many. When it splits, `[[20260623175240]]` (Shards Are Verbatim Gatherings) governs the carve — each shard gathers the verbatim passages for one over-arching concept cluster (non-contiguous, possibly overlapping), under the one hard rule of full coverage. `[[20260623175249]]` (Sharded Children Left Pending) ends the ingest episode on a shard verdict so one context never re-accumulates the bulk; the backlog loop ingests each child later as its own gate-2 episode. `[[20260623175301]]` (Cross-shard Duplicates Tolerated) makes overlap safe — duplicates are reconciled downstream by map-concepts-to-docs and gardening, so shard ingests need no ordering. `[[20260623175312]]` (Sharding Recursion Terminates) bounds the recursion with three floors: an atomic-enough base case, a no-progress guard, and a hard depth cap of 2 tracked by `shard_depth`. And `[[20260623175323]]` (Shard Child Raw Mechanics) records the raw-tier plumbing: each child carries a `parent_raw` pointer (a raw-tier link, not a graph edge) and inherits the parent's provenance unchanged, while the parent stays the immutable whole-archive and derives as `[sharded]` once all children are ingested.
