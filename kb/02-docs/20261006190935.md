## Requirement

Every surface that lists or renders docs shows each doc's standing on the doc's own line, close to the title: what the person did to make the claim exist, and whether the doc is deprecated. The standing is part of how the doc is named in results, not a field the reader has to fetch.

## Why

Results, tooling, and renderings give titles, ids, maybe the type, and maybe a summary, but never the doc's level, so agents fetch and parse whole docs just to learn whether a doc needs to be taken into consideration ([[20261006190927]]). The standing need not be prepended to every mention of a doc, but it belongs on every line that names one.
