## Decision

`force` records how hard a normative claim binds:

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `must` | A rule. Deviation is a conflict. |
| `should` | A guideline. Deviation is allowed with a stated reason. |
| `may` | An allowance. |

It is required on the normative types (principle, constraint, requirement, decision, guide) and forbidden on component, goal, use-case, and reference, whose claims are not norms. Listings show it as a trailing tag on the doc's line.

Force is a facet of its own, separate from intent: who brought a claim forward does not say whether it is a rule or a guideline. The values are the RFC 2119 words, which readers already know.

## Why

A normative doc has to say whether it is a rule or a guideline ([[20261006190931]]).
