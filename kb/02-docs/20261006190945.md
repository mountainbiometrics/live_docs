## Decision

A doc's body may carry an `## Implementation` section describing how its claim is currently built. The section:

- is volatile: it is rewritten whenever the implementation it describes changes;
- is exempt from the rule that docs capture the why rather than the what ([[20260625222613]]);
- is refreshed when the doc's realization is checked against the implementation;
- is never a truth claim: cascade, conflict checks, and other docs do not rely on it.

## Why

People who want implementation details recorded can record them ([[20261006190934]]) in one known place, kept apart from the claim the doc makes.
