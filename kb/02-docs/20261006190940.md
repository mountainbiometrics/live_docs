## Decision

What an agent may do to a doc depends on the doc's intent ([[20261006190938]]):

- **incidental** — the agent revises it freely, with a history note.
- **requested** or **chosen** — the agent never alters it. When the agent's work conflicts with such a claim, it records the conflicting claim as a new incidental doc and flags the conflict for the person.
- **no intent** (a legacy doc nobody has assessed) — treated as chosen until someone assesses it.

## Why

Agents need to iterate on what was only let happen without refusing on the grounds that its shape was ratified ([[20261006190926]]). What the person asked for or picked carries the person's authority, and an agent must not replace it on its own ([[20260723223658]]).
