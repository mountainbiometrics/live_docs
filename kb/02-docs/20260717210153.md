## Decision

Docs with **`type: reference`** or **`status: reference`** are immutable through the porcelain. `ldoc set`, body edits, history appends, and other substantive mutations are refused with a clear warning. Deletion (`ldoc rm`) remains allowed so a bad snapshot can be removed and a fresh one ingested.

Provenance-edge repair that does not rewrite the snapshot body may be permitted when needed to keep citation links honest; rewriting the reference content itself is not.

**Session close respects the same refuse.** History-from-wal materialization at `ldoc session close` skips archived snapshots rather than attempting a creation (or other) history entry that would fail. The review still records the addition from the WAL. Mid-session reference creation — including apply-to-docs Step 1b — must not block close.

## Why

Reference docs are snapshots, not living claims. Editing them in place invites treating them as maintainable documentation and blurs the live_docs vs ordinary-archive distinction. If the snapshot is wrong or irrelevant, delete it and re-snapshot — do not evolve it. Projecting session activity onto their `history:` would be the same in-place mutation under another name.
