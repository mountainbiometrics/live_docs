## Decision

An **async inbox** decouples *capture* from *ingestion*. Raw material can be dropped into an inbox **instantly**, with no processing at capture time. A separate **batch ingest pass** drains the inbox **on demand**, so a queue of accumulated inputs is processed — and reviewed — together.

## Rationale

- **Capture must be frictionless** to actually happen. If ingestion (decomposition into single-responsibility docs) ran synchronously at capture time, the cost of dropping a thought or a link would be high enough to discourage capture, and knowledge would be lost. An instant drop-point removes that friction.
- **Batched draining serves liveness and review** (living-over-stale): material does not rot uncaptured, yet ingestion still applies the full decomposition discipline (ingest-gardening-core) when the pass runs. Processing the inbox as a batch also lets the resulting changes be summarized and reviewed as one coherent unit rather than piecemeal.

## Two-gate refinement

The async model is realized as **three stages separated by two human/skill gates**:

1. **Capture** — `ldoc inbox add`, instant, no gate. Material is dropped willy-nilly.
2. **Gate 1 — promote** — `ldoc promote` is a **human acceptance** step that moves an inbox item into the raw box as an official verbatim **clipping**. This is a deliberate "pull forward," not an automatic drain.
3. **Gate 2 — ingest** — the ingest-reference skill **decomposes** a raw clipping into atomic docs. A raw→docs step can never be a dumb file move, because atomic decomposition is required.

Separating **capture from acceptance from ingestion** is the load-bearing addition: it lets material be dropped without commitment, then pulled forward deliberately by a human, then decomposed with full discipline — each transition gated by judgment rather than happening implicitly.

## Relationship

This decision establishes the *async* model and its two-gate structure. The concrete drop-point, the promote gate, and the ingest pass are specified by the inbox-pipeline component.
