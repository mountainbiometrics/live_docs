## Use case

A checker, validate or an agent, looks at a parent and has to tell two cases apart. A heading asserts nothing but the grouping of its children, so the why-rule exception applies to it. A parent of any other type may also have children, and it still carries a why, because it also makes a claim.

## Why

The store keeps a hierarchy so any type can sit above other docs, and it excuses only the parent that exists to group them ([[20260625222638]]). A checker can honor both when the claimless parent is declared, which is how a reader tells from the doc's line whether the doc is a claim to weigh ([[20261006190927]]).
