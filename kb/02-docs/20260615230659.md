## Context

Ids are opaque and titles are descriptive but long, so neither is a friendly handle for humans or agents referencing docs in commands and skill steps. A paragraph-length overview of a doc is also needed in many places (reviews, CLI output, navigation) and must be available as structured data.

## Decision

Every doc carries **three human-facing descriptors**:

- **`label`** — a short (2–5 word) Title-Case identifier; a compact handle friendlier than an opaque id but less verbose than the title.
- **`title`** — a sentence-length descriptive name.
- **`summary`** — a 2–5 sentence overview, held in **frontmatter**.

### Label

A label is non-empty, trimmed, unique across the store (case-insensitive), and matches `^[A-Za-z0-9]+([ -][A-Za-z0-9]+)*$` — letters and digits with single spaces or hyphens between tokens. Whitespace IS allowed, so `label-and-title` and `porcelain api` are both valid; kebab-case remains a special case. Resolution accepts id, exact label, exact title, or a unique case-insensitive substring of title or label — whichever the caller finds most natural. Multi-word labels are quoted on the CLI.

When `ldoc new` (or `KB.new`) is given no `--label`, one is auto-derived from the title: take whole words up to ~24 characters, breaking only on word boundaries (never mid-word), strip trailing punctuation. The result is **Title Case** (each word capitalised), NOT kebab-cased — inter-word spaces are preserved and the casing convention makes labels read as names, not slugs. On collision, ` 2`, ` 3`, … is appended.

#### Label casing convention

A label is a short, human-readable **Title-Case** name — whitespace is allowed and words are capitalised. Examples: `Porcelain API`, `Batch Operations`, `Label and Title`. Kebab slugs (e.g. `porcelain-api`) are valid syntax but are the exceptional case, not the norm. Autogeneration always produces Title Case from word boundaries; hand-written labels should also follow Title Case.

### Summary

The `summary` is a **real frontmatter field**. It is the single source for overview snapshots used in review output, `ldoc` display, search/navigation blurbs, and future MOC maps. It must not be tied to a body-section parsing rule.

## Consequences

- Humans and agents reference docs by label in `ldoc` commands and prose.
- Display labels stay compact and human-readable (the label is scannable; the title is readable in full context).
- Titles stay descriptive without needing to be terse.
- Allowing whitespace means auto-generated labels read as natural phrases rather than hyphenated slugs.
- Title Case makes labels visually distinct from both ids and arbitrary prose, reducing ambiguity in summaries and review output.
- The summary is available as structured data to every tool and skill, decoupled from body formatting.
