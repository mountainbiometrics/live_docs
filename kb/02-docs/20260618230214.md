The bedrock principles every live_docs decision derives from. Start here to understand why the system is shaped the way it is.

## Map

- [Living over Stale](20260615100001.md) — the prime directive: docs track current reality, and drift is the enemy worse than ignorance.
- [Dependency Tracking](20260615100002.md) — make drift checkable by recording explicit dependencies that trigger cascade checks.
- [Single Responsibility](20260615100003.md) — one reason to change per doc; a wide cascade is the signal to split.
- [Cognitive Load](20260615100004.md) — navigate by signpost and selective depth, not front-loaded comprehensiveness.
- [Maintainability](20260615100005.md) — tooling maintains the docs; systems relying on human discipline alone fail.
- [Tool Agnostic](20260615100000.md) — plain markdown on a filesystem; git and Obsidian are optional conveniences, not requirements.
