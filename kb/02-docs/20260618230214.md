## What this component covers

Foundational Principles holds the bedrock layer every live_docs design decision derives from — the shared axioms, plus the boundaries and directional stance they imply. These are not operational instructions; they are what rules out bad designs and justifies the unusual choices in the rest of the system.

## The core methodological pair

The two principles that most tightly govern what live_docs is for:

**Capture the why, not the what** ([[20260625222613]]): docs record the nonrecoverable why-web — principles, constraints, requirements, goals, and the decision process that weighed them — the information the code cannot express. Code already holds the what; decisions are often recoverable too. Prefer thin decisions that require those roots. A doc that merely restates the what (or an outcome without why) is a defect. live_docs is a higher-resolution lens into why, not a design-doc replica.

**Docs as code** ([[20260625222626]]): design knowledge is maintained with the same discipline as well-factored code — atomically decomposed, single-responsibility, precisely editable. The opposite of monolithic design documents that must be replaced in bulk.

**Every doc carries its why** ([[20260625222638]]) is the operational enforcement of the first: a doc that states only a what, with no why, is repaired or removed. Pure signposts are the one exception.

The directional counterpart to capturing the why is **Docs lead, code aligns** ([[20260707164539]]): because the why is not recoverable from code, the docs are the source of truth and the code reconciles to them — which is exactly what makes a doc/code mismatch a detectable signal rather than an impossibility.

## The six original principles

The original six principles ([[20260615100000]] through [[20260615100005]]) establish the system's structural axioms: tool-agnosticism (functionality lives in scripts, not in Obsidian or git), living-over-stale (show what is, not what was), explicit dependency tracking (cascade is the enforcement mechanism), single responsibility (one doc, one reason to change), cognitive load management (hierarchy as selective depth, not front-loaded comprehensiveness), and maintainability (tooling maintains the docs, not human discipline alone).

## Later refinements

Three principles added from operational experience:

**Singular ownership** ([[20260624172648]]): single responsibility means exactly one doc owns a claim — not just one topic. Diffuse ownership (multiple docs each partially covering a claim) means none is the real owner; collapse is the repair.

**Agentic inconsistency** ([[20260624172719]]): multi-agent pipelines produce intermittent, non-deterministic inconsistency — a structural property. Sampling QA is the correct response; exhaustive full-scan validation catches schema errors, not this class of problem.

**Shared text vs. procedure** ([[20260624172737]]): sub-skills handle shared procedure (steps every caller runs identically); prose includes handle shared text (a policy each caller reads and applies with judgment). Using a sub-skill for a definition is wrong — it obscures that the consumer is just reading a policy.

**Incidental conventions** ([[20260618214128]]): borrow prior-art conventions only for incidental structure. Where this system has already made a deliberate decision, that decision overrides.

## What the system is for, and what keeps it living

**Intent-bearing knowledge** ([[20260707005524]]): the system's job is keeping intent-bearing knowledge — the why behind a system — true as reality changes, a write-time concern. Retrieving or serving it is a separate, read-time layer over a store that is already true.

**Harness-agnostic** ([[20260707164524]]) follows directly: the agent harness and retrieval layers (RAG, MCP, prompt-assembly) are downstream and swappable, and the store commits to none of them. Being unopinionated about the harness is a consequence of that boundary, not a gap.

**Surgical regeneration** ([[20260707005515]]): the store is *kept* true by regenerating — cascade rewrites each stale dependent to current truth from the graph, surgically and only the affected atomic node, never a wholesale rebuild and never a snapshot left to rot. Atomicity is what makes the local rewrite safe.

**Revisable decisions** ([[20260707170659]]): a decision is safely revisable only because its why-web and its dependencies are recorded — which is what lets the rulebook *evolve in place* rather than ossify or be quietly ignored. When challenged, re-evaluation needs those roots as a fair checklist. Enforcement ("does this change conflict with a prior decision?") is one affordance of that graph, not its whole job.
