The bedrock principles every live_docs decision derives from. Start here to understand why the system is shaped the way it is.

Begin with `[[20260615100001]]` (Living over Stale), the prime directive: docs must track current reality, and drift is the enemy worse than ignorance. `[[20260615100002]]` (Dependency Tracking) makes that drift checkable by recording explicit dependencies that trigger cascade checks, and `[[20260615100003]]` (Single Responsibility) keeps each doc to one reason to change — a wide cascade being the signal to split. `[[20260615100004]]` (Cognitive Load) shapes navigation around signposts and selective depth rather than front-loaded comprehensiveness, while `[[20260615100005]]` (Maintainability) insists the tooling maintain the docs, since systems relying on human discipline alone fail. Underpinning all of it, `[[20260615100000]]` (Tool Agnostic) keeps the store plain markdown on a filesystem, with git and Obsidian optional conveniences rather than requirements.
