## Summary

Land Integrated Lexicon sibling system in KB: unnumbered lexicon box, term-slug identity, record shape (no on-disk id), soft term links, viewer Lexicon view, and ldoc term porcelain — aligned with shipped implementation.

## Additions
- [[20260716222957]]
  A configurable lexicon box of human-ratified term records outside the doc graph. Soft sync both ways: the KB suggests candidates; the lexicon guides wording without mandating rewrites. Humans are the primary audience for entries.
- [[20260716223013]]
  The lexicon lives in a configurable box key lexicon (default kb/lexicon/ under base), the same unnumbered class as reviews and sessions — not a numbered distillation stage, and outside the doc graph.
- [[20260716223014]]
  A term's identity is its filesystem path under the lexicon box: lexicon/<term>.md or lexicon/<context>/<term>.md (kebab-case lowercase). Rename means delete and recreate; references are not backfilled.
- [[20260716223015]]
  On-disk term frontmatter is term, definition, optional context, allowed_aliases, restricted_aliases, similar_terms, and created — omit empty. Identity is the filesystem path; no id key, status, cascade participation, or history.
- [[20260716223016]]
  Explicit term links use the [[…]] family, distinct from 14-digit doc ids: [[Cascade]], [[(Graph) Cascade]], or [[graph/cascade]]. Broken links are useful; renames do not rewrite old references.
- [[20260716223017]]
  A term's optional context field disambiguates homonyms within the lexicon. It is not the doc domain facet and is not inherited, filtered, or cascaded as domain tags are.
- [[20260716223018]]
  A top-level Lexicon page in the HTML viewer (not a Catalog tab): lists terms, resolves explicit soft term links with hover definitions, and surfaces cheap usage stats from doc backlinks.
- [[20260716223019]]
  ldoc exposes term verbs over the lexicon sibling box: new, get, show, ls, find, set, and rm. Identity changes are refused in place; delete and recreate instead.
