## Additions
- [[20260701224818]]
- [[20260701224958]]
  Creation is recorded as a doc's first history entry (an addition), superseding the rule that history excluded creation. The separate created field is dropped — creation time is the addition entry's timestamp — via a one-shot migration, pure back-compat reads, and heal-on-next-write.
- [[20260701224959]]
  Every store mutation runs inside an open session; if none is set, ldoc auto-creates one and announces it rather than refusing. Setting a session id over one already open is an error; fragmentation from un-exported auto-creates is reconciled by session merge.
- [[20260701224960]]
  An open session is a record in a configurable sessions/ box, keyed by id, holding its settable agent-recap summary and change log (WAL). At close it rolls into exactly one review and is deleted; the raw WAL is preserved losslessly in a non-rendered block of that review.
- [[20260701225016]]
  Every mutation double-writes: a per-doc history entry and a line in the open session's write-ahead log. The WAL is the source of truth for the review and the only place deletions are recorded, capturing op, ref, the change_type list, and note.
- [[20260701225017]]
  Change explanations are attached inline via --note on the mutating command, making note and change atomic. Obvious commands auto-fill their note; the standalone ldoc history verb is deprecated to a post-hoc gap-filler.
- [[20260701225029]]
  session close blocks until every doc that had a revision-class change carries at least one author-supplied note; per-command a missing note only nags. Auto-notes do not discharge a revision, and one author note per doc suffices.
