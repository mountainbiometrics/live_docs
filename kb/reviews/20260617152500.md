## Additions
- [[20260617144333]]
  A design session exploring live_docs graph structure and edge-type vocabulary. Started from dissatisfaction with exploration feeling clunky (no summaries, no signposts, no cluster identity), and converged on edge-typing as the keystone fix.
- [[20260617144542]]
- [[20260617144556]]
  The original live_docs spec described an 'influence/impact web' where cyclic relationships are meaningful. The word 'dependency' in early implementation language silently imposed DAG semantics — acyclicity at the graph level. Since a DAG subsumes hierarchy, the 'hierarchy' feature appeared to be the only thing deferred, while the intended web was dropped without being named.
- [[20260617144634]]
  The existing schema had only two edge types: depends_on (structural dependency) and references (provenance). As the graph grew, the need for cluster edges (non-cascading kinship) and proper structural hierarchy became clear. The design session iterated over a behavioral matrix to derive the minimal closed vocabulary.
- [[20260617144637]]
  The decision in 20260615221004 established 'references' as the provenance edge (distinct from structural depends_on). The name 'references' is a generic-sounding word that obscures its behavioral distinction — exactly the pattern that caused the DAG/web confusion with 'dependency'. During the edge-typing design session, the question arose whether 'references' (provenance) and a new 'relates' (cluster) edge could be collapsed into one type.
