## Additions
- [[20260623174829]]
  The shard-clipping (gate 1.5) design: a pre-pass between promote and ingest-reference that divides the unit of work, not the phases. Oversized clippings have their verbatim passages gathered per concept cluster into pending child raws; ingest stays whole per shard.
- [[20260623175215]]
  When a clipping overflows one ingest context, shrink the unit of work into smaller verbatim units each ingested whole — never split a single ingest into a plan-phase and an apply-phase. Comprehension and synthesis are inseparable for one conceptual unit.
- [[20260623175228]]
  shard-clipping is a pipeline stage between promote (gate 1) and ingest-reference (gate 2), invoked automatically by ingest-reference as its first sub-step. It decides whether a promoted raw clipping is one gate-2 unit or many, gathering verbatim passages into pending child raws when oversized.
- [[20260623175240]]
  A shard gathers the verbatim source passages for one over-arching concept cluster. Passages are non-contiguous and may overlap between shards, and are never rephrased. The one hard rule: every passage lands in at least one shard — full coverage.
- [[20260623175249]]
  When shard-clipping returns a shard verdict, ingest-reference leaves the child raws pending and ends — it does not enumerate or loop over them. One context never re-accumulates the bulk. The backlog loop over pending raws ingests each child later as its own gate-2 episode.
- [[20260623175301]]
  Duplicate concepts arising across shards are not prevented at split time; they are reconciled downstream by map-concepts-to-docs' concept-matching and the gardening passes. Because reconciliation is downstream, shard ingests need no ordering or serialization.
- [[20260623175312]]
  Sharding recursion is bounded by three floors: an atomic-enough base case that halts, a no-progress guard that passes material through when it won't separate into at least two clusters, and a hard depth cap of 2 levels tracked by the shard_depth field.
- [[20260623175323]]
  Each child raw carries a parent_raw path pointer (a raw-tier pointer, NOT a graph edge — raw files are not graph nodes) and inherits the parent's provenance unchanged. The parent raw stays the immutable whole-archive and derives as [sharded] once all children are ingested.
