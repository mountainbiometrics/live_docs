## Additions

- [[20260624185845]]
  The set of domain tags in a store is a finite shared resource, curated qualitatively rather than by volume. A domain earns its place only by pulling its own weight as a distinct, filterable grouping — not by spanning a number of scopes. The right count of domains depends entirely on the documented system; the discipline is that each one justifies itself, conventions stay internally consistent, and over-eager tagging is resisted.
- [[20260624185902]]
  Domains emerge from use, not a seed: a store ships with zero and never presumes a user's domains. They are originated at tag time — the writer applies any applicable domains but is nudged to reuse an existing one (listed via a domain registry, e.g. ldoc domains) over inventing. Gardening refines the vocabulary later. Ingest originates, gardening refines — the same division as belongs_to placement.
