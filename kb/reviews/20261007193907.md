## Summary

Record a heading type for a claimless parent, leaving the signpost role on descendants.

## Additions
- [[20261007193650]]
  A checker can tell a heading, whose only assertion is the grouping of its children, from a parent that also makes a claim. The why-rule exception applies to the heading. Every other parent still carries a why.
- [[20261007193651]]
  The why-rule's exception for a parent that only groups its children is its own doc type. A checker reads the type. A parent that also makes a claim keeps its type and still carries a why.
- [[20261007193652]]
  A heading is one kind of signpost: a parent that asserts nothing but its grouping. Any type may have children, and overview, entry points, recombination, listings, and cascade keep following descendants.
- [[20261007193653]]
  The claimless parent is a heading. It carries intent, forbids force, realization, and imposed_by, expects no why-edge, and validate requires one living child. The name is slightly unfamiliar, so it stays rare.
