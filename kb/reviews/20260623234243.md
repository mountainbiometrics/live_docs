## Additions

- [[20260623233906]]
  What makes a good belongs_to placement is defined once as shared prose and consumed verbatim by both the doc-creating writer and the gardening refiner, so the two cannot drift. Prefer the nearest coherent signpost, one primary parent, the right-sized branch — and defer rather than force a bad home.
- [[20260623233922]]
  Gardening is realized as a thin dispatcher over a family of single-purpose, individually-tunable phases. The dispatcher owns the whole episode — one START, the routing decision, one cascade over the union of changes, one review — while each phase does one job and only reports the ids it changed.
- [[20260623233935]]
  keywords is a flat, optional, non-inherited, non-cascade frontmatter list of search synonyms with no governance bar, existing purely for findability. It is distinct from domain (a governed business-grouping facet): ldoc find term-matches keywords, and new/set accept --keywords with replace semantics.
- [[20260623233949]]
  The ingest/apply writer originates a doc's belongs_to placement at birth — giving it a semi-adequate parent from the best visible signpost using the shared placement policy — while gardening refines placements later with full-store context. Ingest originates; gardening refines, rather than originating every placement.
