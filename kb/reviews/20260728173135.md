## Summary

Corrected modularity breakdown: High Cohesion is its own principle under Docs as Code; supports SRP, does not equal it

## Additions
- [[20260728173103]]
  A document has high cohesion when every part of it relates to one concept. That relatedness supports single responsibility but is not the same claim — cohesion is about content sticking together; single responsibility is about one reason to change.
