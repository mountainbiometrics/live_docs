Session: removed the `keywords` frontmatter field from live_docs entirely.

What happened: keywords was a flat, optional, non-inherited, ungoverned
findability-synonym list, distinct in intent from the governed `domain`
facet (domain was meant as a curated, filterable business-grouping facet;
keywords was meant as free-text search aliases with no curation). In
practice the distinction never earned its keep: keywords was rarely
populated, never meaningfully governed despite garden-refine nominally
curating it, and any real synonym/aliasing need overlapped with what
`domain` (or plain body prose) already covered. It was a second axis
answering a question `domain` + full-text search already answered well
enough, so it was schema surface without payoff.

Decision: remove `keywords` completely rather than keep it around
unused. This is a pure subtraction — no renamed/replacement field.
Removed from: CLI flags (--keywords / --tags-keywords) on `new`/`set`,
the `find` term-matching and snippet logic, the canonical field order
and YAML emission, the tag-normalization/validation loop, and the
change-type-taxonomy field mapping. The 7 kb/02-docs docs that had a
populated `keywords:` field had it stripped mechanically.

Why this matters for future intent: WISH.md separately floats an
"Integrated Lexicon" wishlist item (a store-hosted box of canonical
term records, aliases, definitions) — that is an unbuilt future idea
exploring a much more deliberate, curated approach to vocabulary, not
a like-for-like replacement for the removed `keywords` field. Nothing
about this removal should be read as decided in favor of, or blocked
on, that future idea.
