This conversation surfaced that agents were incorrectly treating 'deprecate' as the only way to retire a doc in live_docs. The actual intent: deletion is the right choice for duplicates and content-free redundancy; deprecation is for preserving historical decisions that were later overturned. The reference skill was updated to make delete-vs-deprecate explicit, with deletion as the default when no information would be lost.
