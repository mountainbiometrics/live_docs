Articulate the difference between a dependent that is a child vs one that is not (required_by). Clarify which words/terms are okay to use in each situation. Children are reverse belongs_to (structural membership / hierarchy). Required_by is reverse requires (logical dependency without structural membership). The conflated 'dependents' label was a glaring issue — now split into 'required_by' and 'children' in the CLI/API.
