## Decision

There is **no confidence-gated auto-bless**. The system does not automatically approve (or skip review of) a change because some confidence score is high. Review is about confirming **intent and securing signoff**, not about measuring confidence.

## Rationale

A confidence threshold would smuggle a *quality gate* back in through the side door and conflate two unrelated things: how *sure* a model is about a change versus whether a *reviewer endorses its intent*. High confidence in a wrong intent is exactly the failure review is meant to catch. Since changes are already blessed on creation (review-is-post-hoc), there is nothing for an auto-bless to unblock — it would only suppress the very review signal we want.

## How burden is actually reduced

Review burden is reduced by making summaries **cleaner and more digestible** (see review-the-summary, cognitive-load) — better grouping, sharper one-line intents, label-keyed lines — **never** by skipping review or auto-approving. The lever is comprehension, not omission.
