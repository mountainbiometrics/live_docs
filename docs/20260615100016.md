## Context

Raw inputs to a documentation system — meeting notes, Slack threads, code comments, external articles — are messy, multi-topic, and do not arrive pre-factored. Separately, existing docs drift and accumulate technical debt. These problems could be addressed by dedicated "ingest" and "gardening" skills, but they could also be deferred as nice-to-haves.

## Decision

Ingest and gardening skills are foundational — they ship with v1, not deferred. They are the decomposition engine: where messy external reality and accumulated drift get factored into single-responsibility units. They must encode the single-responsibility discipline (the "two reasons to change → split" test), not merely move content.

## Consequences

The system is useful from day one for converting raw material into well-factored docs (P4). Without ingest, the corpus fills with multi-responsibility blobs that undermine the whole design. Without gardening, drift accumulates silently (P2). These skills are the highest-leverage investment: they prevent the quality problems that make documentation systems get abandoned (P6).
