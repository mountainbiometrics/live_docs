## Context

idea_dump proposed two tag dimensions: `scope` (which project/service/module a doc applies to) and `domain` (grouping by business domain, e.g. Account Management, Ontology Mapping). `scope` is well-defined by the live_docs-vs-sinai portability decision. `domain` was introduced into the frontmatter schema alongside `scope` but without a formal definition of its semantics, vocabulary, or relationship to scope.

## Decision

`tags.domain` groups a doc by the business or problem domain it belongs to, independent of which repo or service it pertains to. Examples: `Account Management`, `Ontology Mapping`, `Confidence Reporting`. The vocabulary is open (not an enum) and grows as domains are identified. An empty list (`domain: []`) means the doc applies system-wide or the domain is not yet named.

## Consequences

Domain tags enable filtering and indexing by business concern across scope boundaries. A doc scoped to `AnnotatorAgent` might also be tagged `domain: [Ontology Mapping]`. The open vocabulary means no governance overhead, but a gardening pass should periodically normalize tags to prevent synonym drift (e.g. `Account Mgmt` vs `Account Management`). Domain tags are optional and advisory — they do not drive cascade checks.
