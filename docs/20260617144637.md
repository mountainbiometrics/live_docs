## Context

The decision in 20260615221004 established 'references' as the provenance edge (distinct from structural depends_on). The name 'references' is a generic-sounding word that obscures its behavioral distinction — exactly the pattern that caused the DAG/web confusion with 'dependency'. During the edge-typing design session, the question arose whether 'references' (provenance) and a new 'relates' (cluster) edge could be collapsed into one type.

## Decision

**Rename the field**: 'references' → 'provenance'. The name 'provenance' is honest — it names what the edge *does* (records ingest lineage), not a generic 'references something'.

**Do not collapse relates and provenance.** Three functional differences survive:

1. **Direction is the information.** Provenance is 'A was derived from B' — the arrow is the content (B is the source, A the derivative). 'relates' is symmetric. Collapsing erases the source/derivative ordering.

2. **Immutable fact vs. living judgment.** Provenance records what happened at ingest; it does not change when the source changes and survives the source going historical. 'relates' is a current assessment; garden prunes stale relates edges but must *never* prune a provenance edge (you cannot un-derive something).

3. **validate enforces provenance invariants.** Every doc extracted by ingest-reference is required to carry a provenance edge; validate checks this. No analogous structural invariant exists for relates — it is purely optional navigation.

## Consequences

- All docs currently using 'references' in frontmatter should be migrated to 'provenance' via a bulk garden pass.
- The ingest-reference skill's --references flag becomes --provenance (or the skill is updated to write provenance edges).
- The decision in 20260615221004 remains valid for the behavioral distinction; this decision records the rename and the non-collapse rationale.
