## Purpose

A `principle` doc captures a durable, defensible rule of thumb that guides decisions and designs. Principles are directional, not prescriptive — they say "prefer X over Y" and explain why.

## Required Fields

All canonical baseline fields. No type-specific additions. Level should be `preference` or higher once a principle is genuinely held; `incidental` is appropriate for calcified habits not yet consciously chosen.

## Lifecycle

`living` while the principle is actively held. `historical` when superseded or revoked — a retired principle is still useful context for understanding past decisions.

## Cascade Behavior

When a principle changes, `cascade-check` should examine:
- `decision` docs that cite this principle in `depends_on`.
- `guide` docs whose guidance is grounded in this principle.

A wide cascade from a principle change is a design smell: either the principle was too broad (should be split) or too many docs coupled to it directly rather than to the decisions it informed.
