## Purpose

A `requirement` doc records something the system must do or provide because an external party — a business stakeholder, compliance body, contract, or external dependency — demands it. Distinguished from `constraint` (environmental limitation) by its origin: requirements come from people or organisations, constraints come from the environment.

## Required Fields

All canonical baseline fields. No type-specific additions.

## Lifecycle

`living` while the obligation holds. `historical` when the requirement is fulfilled, waived, or superseded — record the reason in `history`. Never delete: the history of obligations explains past design choices.

## Cascade Behavior

When a requirement changes, `cascade-check` should examine:
- `decision` docs that `depends_on` this requirement — do they still address it?
- `use-case` docs whose flows were designed to satisfy this requirement.

A requirement that cascades to many docs in a routine update was probably too coarse. Split it into specific, independently satisfiable obligations.
