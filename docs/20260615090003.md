## Purpose

A `decision` doc records a significant choice — why it was made, what it forecloses, and what it enables. It is the primary vehicle for architectural decision records (ADRs) and any other choice worth explaining to a future reader.

## Required Fields

All canonical baseline fields. No type-specific additions.

## Lifecycle

`living` while the decision stands. When reversed or superseded, set `status: historical` and add a `history` entry citing the new decision. Never delete: past decisions explain why things are the way they are.

## Cascade Behavior

When a decision changes, `cascade-check` should examine:
- `component`, `guide`, and `use-case` docs that `depends_on` this decision — do their implementations still match?
- `requirement` or `constraint` docs that this decision was a response to — does the decision still satisfy them?

A decision doc should own exactly one choice. If changing it cascades to more than two or three docs, the decision was probably bundling multiple choices and should be split.
